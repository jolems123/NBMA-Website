# NBMA Regional Media — Attribution and Licensing Register

The website credits third-party media in three places: on each photo or video (bottom-left credit line), in the site footer (hero video), and on the public **/media-credits** page. All three are generated from the `media` register in `site.js`; update that register first when media changes.

Media is served remotely from Wikimedia Commons and is **not bundled** in the repository. Use does not imply that the people, businesses or creators shown are associated with NBMA or endorse it. Images and video are cropped responsively to fill the screen.
